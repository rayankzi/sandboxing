Row Space and Column Space

Rank, nullity, and when \(A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}\) has a solution

Row space and column space

Definition

Let \(A\) be an \(m\times n\) matrix.

  • The subspace of \(\mathbb{R}^n\) spanned by the row vectors of \(A\) is the Row Space of \(A\).
  • The subspace of \(\mathbb{R}^m\) spanned by the column vectors of \(A\) is the Column Space of \(A\).
notes p. 1

Example 2 — a column space that is a line

\[A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix},\qquad \mathbf{a}_2 = 2\mathbf{a}_1\]

So the column space is \(\operatorname{span}\!\left(\begin{bmatrix}1\\2\end{bmatrix}\right)\), the line \(L.\)

Instructor note \(\operatorname{span}\!\left(\begin{bmatrix}1\\2\end{bmatrix},\begin{bmatrix}2\\4\end{bmatrix}\right)=\operatorname{span}\!\left(\begin{bmatrix}1\\2\end{bmatrix}\right)=\) line \(L\)
